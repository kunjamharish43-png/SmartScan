# Privacy Policy for Smart Scan

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Smart Scan is developed by Harish Gond. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Smart Scan Android application.

## 1. Local Storage & Scanned Documents
- Scanned images, PDF files, extracted OCR text, and folders are stored locally on your device.
- App lock (biometric fingerprint or device screen lock) uses your device's built-in authentication system.

## 2. AI Document Assistant
- When you use AI features (such as summarizing, translating, or asking questions), your selected prompt and relevant document text may be sent over the internet to configured AI service providers to generate a response.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
- The app displays banner and interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob. Advertising partners may collect device identifiers or ad interaction data according to their privacy policies.

## 4. Permissions
- **Camera:** Used to scan documents and ID cards.
- **Storage / Files:** Used to import images/PDFs and save or share exported files.
- **Internet:** Used for AI assistant responses and ad delivery.
