# Privacy Policy for Smart Scan

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Smart Scan is developed by Harish Gond. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Smart Scan Android application.

## 1. Local Storage & Scanned Documents
- Scanned images, PDF files, extracted OCR text, and folders are stored locally on your device.
- App lock (biometric fingerprint or device screen lock) uses your device's built-in authentication system. We do not collect or store your biometric data.

## 2. AI Document Assistant
- When you use AI features (such as summarizing, translating, or asking questions), your selected prompt and relevant document text may be sent over the internet to configured AI service providers to generate a response.

## 3. Advertising (Start.io & Third-Party Ad Networks)
- The app displays banner and interstitial advertisements through **Start.io** (and may use Google AdMob).
- Advertising partners may collect device identifiers, IP address, or ad interaction data to serve and measure ads according to their own privacy policies:
  - Start.io Privacy Policy: https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/
  - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Permissions
- **Camera:** Used to scan documents and ID cards.
- **Storage / Files:** Used to import images/PDFs and save or share exported files.
- **Biometric:** Used for optional app lock security.
- **Notifications:** Used to show document processing or export status.
- **Internet:** Used for AI assistant responses and ad delivery.
